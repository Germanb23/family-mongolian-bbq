Tapo St

Park out front. Walk into the glow.

A tile-roofed storefront in the Tapo Street plaza, lanterns lit at dusk. Through the door: long rows of navy tufted booths under amber stained-glass pendants, the sauce bar and grill station along the back wall. A neighborhood dining room since 2013.

Grill

The Mongolian BBQ grill · Dine-in

You build the bowl. We fire the grill.

Start with noodles or rice, pile on thin-sliced beef, pork, shrimp or tofu, load up fresh vegetables, then finish with the house sauces and oils. It goes onto the grill and comes back hot.

  1. 1
    Pick a baseFresh noodles or steamed rice
  2. 2
    Choose your proteinsSliced beef, pork, shrimp, calamari, tofu
  3. 3
    Load the vegetablesBroccoli, cabbage, onions, mushrooms, sprouts, peppers, jalapeños and more
  4. 4
    Sauce it, grill itHouse sauces, garlic, chili, sesame, grilled fresh

The grill is a dine-in experience at Tapo Street. Everything on the full menu is available for pickup and delivery.
